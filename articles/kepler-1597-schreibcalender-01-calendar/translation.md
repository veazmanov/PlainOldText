# Writing Calendar for the Year 1598

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the calendar as printed, made for Plain Old Text. Saints' days and feasts are given in their usual English forms; the weather is translated, and a word the printer divided between two days is given whole in the row where it begins. The marks, the Moon's places and the times of the Moon's phases are as in the original; *V.* and *N.* are hours before and after noon.
:::

![](/img/schreibcalender-1598/title.webp){alt="Photograph of the title page, with the arms of Styria in a woodcut border" data-label="Title page"}

Writing Calendar for the Year after the Birth of the Lord Christ our Redeemer, 1598. Set out by M. Johannes Kepler, Mathematician to the Honourable Estates of the Duchy of Styria.

Printed at Graz in Styria by Hans Schmidt.

![](/img/schreibcalender-1598/title-verso.webp){alt="Photograph of the page giving the year's reckonings and the signs of the planets" data-label="Title verso"}

In the year after the birth and gracious incarnation of Jesus Christ, our only Redeemer and Saviour, reckoned 1598. From the creation of the world 5560. From the Flood 3904. The second after the leap year.

The golden number 3.\
Epact or addition 23.\
The Roman indiction 11.\
Sunday letter D.

Between Christmas and Shrovetide, 5 weeks 3 days.

The movable and immovable feasts, with the Gospels, the length of the day and other significations, are found in order in the following calendar.
