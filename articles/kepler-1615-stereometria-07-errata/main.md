# Errata

::: {.editorial-note}
Spelling, accents and italics as printed, including *v* for *u*. Long s is given as s, and the abbreviation *q;* is written out as *que*. The errata are printed on a slip pasted onto the last page of text (page 112), which in this copy unfolds beside it; the slip has no page number of its own. Its references are to signatures and leaves, not to page numbers: "Pag. D. 2. fac. b." is the second leaf of gathering D, its verso (*facies b*), and "l." is *lege*, "read". Each entry gives the printed words, a dash for the words between, and the correction, in the abbreviated form of the original. The two columns of the slip are given one after the other.
:::
