# Bartsch to Müller, 30 April 1629

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the corresponding extract of the original; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

… As for me: since in recent months I have dealt a great deal with Mr Kepler, in person and by letter, about publishing our *Ephemerides*, I have at last decided to hand them over to him, computed at first for 10 years, so that a first volume of *Ephemerides* can be published as soon as possible. And he, after looking for various chances of printing in various places, has at last decided too to bring them out at Frankfurt through Tampach, who will refund the costs. So, as both of them thought, a volume of our *Ephemerides* from the year 1617 will come out at the coming autumn fair, with the 3 others already printed, up to the year 1636, or at any rate to 1633 inclusive. And this is the chief reason why I am forced to press on with the calculation night and day, together with my brother, and cannot attend to anything else. Mr Kepler has indeed published his reply for this reason, which I had otherwise meant to send on this occasion as well. …

I should not like to refuse Mr Kepler my partnership, since, besides other promises not to be despised, he himself pays no small reward for the labour. We are planning more, but everything is still uncertain. As soon as anything certain is decided about the rest, I shall not hide it from you. …
