# Crüger to Müller, 4/14 April 1624

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the extract beside it; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

Why then, you say, should we not embrace Kepler's astronomy? I rightly admire this man's acumen and subtlety. But not everything sharp is also straight, as Lipsius says somewhere … So I do not subscribe to Mr Kepler's hypotheses. *God will, I hope, give another way of finding the true theory of Mars.*
