# Crüger to Müller, 31 December 1634

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the extract beside it; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

About Kepler's posthumous writings, and the rest, I have a commission from our Council to find out what those writings are like and what they are offered at. So, since there is an easier chance from Leipzig than from Danzig to Sagan, please get me a catalogue of the writings that can be sold, and, if Tycho's observations are among them, find out whether the widow is also free to sell them, since without doubt the Emperor could claim them for himself, as only entrusted to Kepler. Please take this matter seriously, and if not by this chance (which indeed cannot be done) at least by another, let me know about it all.
