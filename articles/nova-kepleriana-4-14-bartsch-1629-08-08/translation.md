# Bartsch to Müller, 29 July/8 August 1629

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the corresponding extract of the original; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

Two days ago . . I sent a copy of Kepler's *Sportula*, printed only a few days before with the author's own type. Now I add the letter left out then for lack of time, and say only this one thing: that at the next fair, God helping, I mean to visit your Muses again, and to set out better in person what I ought perhaps to write now, and what you might be glad to know. For since the first volume of our *Ephemerides*, containing 20 years, from 17 to 37, is to be published at Frankfurt as soon as possible, on the advice of Mr Kepler, who lately lived here with me, I intend to go there, for many reasons to be explained in person, and from there to go back to Strasbourg on my own business; and in passing (as I said) more with you in person. …
