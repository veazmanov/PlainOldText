# Bartsch to Müller, 7/17 November 1630

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the corresponding extract of the original; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

… Since my lord father-in-law (long known to you from his writings, and now in person and by conversation; may God bring him back safe and sound to you, to me, to others, to all) has left me the power meanwhile to print here whatever I like that suits astronomical use; and since he long ago so agreed and advised, and I have so far tried various things and deliberated, I have agreed with the printer to print in a smaller format (12^mo^) some astronomical-logistic tables, of both the first and the second movables, that make the doctrine and use of the Rudolphine Tables easier, or at least shorter. …
