# Crüger to Müller, August 1623

::: {.editorial-note}
Only the Latin is given; the editors' German commentary and notes are left out. The 1927 edition prints only extracts. Each paragraph below is one of its extracts, and the omission marks (…) are the editors'. Spaced type, which the editors used for emphasis, is given in italics. [Magini?, Galilei?] is the editors' conjecture.
:::

Gratias ago pro communicatis Kepplerianis. Mirabundria sunt (aiebat Italus iste [Magini?, Galilei?]) ut vir ipse. Videor mihi, quotiescunque talia et alia Keppleri lego, nescio quorsum extra me abripi. Seposui haec obscura ad tempus, cum mihi bellum e bello surgat.
