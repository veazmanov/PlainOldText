# Crüger to Müller, August 1623

::: {.editorial-note}
From Peter Crüger's letter to Philipp Müller of August 1623, written at Danzig, thanking him for writings of Kepler's that Müller had sent. Crüger quotes an unnamed Italian on Kepler; the 1927 editors suggest Magini or Galileo, with a query. The original is in the Bibliothèque de l'Observatoire, Paris (B, 1, 9; 89, 9. J. 31). Printed in extracts in *Nova Kepleriana* 4, ed. Walther von Dyck and Max Caspar, Abhandlungen der Bayerischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Math.-naturw. Abteilung 31, no. 1 (Munich, 1927), 108.

Transcribed from the page images of the 1927 printing. Only the Latin is given; the editors' German commentary and notes are left out. The 1927 edition prints only extracts. Each paragraph below is one of its extracts, and the omission marks (…) are the editors'. Spaced type, which the editors used for emphasis, is given in italics. [Magini?, Galilei?] is the editors' conjecture.
:::

Gratias ago pro communicatis Kepplerianis. Mirabundria sunt (aiebat Italus iste [Magini?, Galilei?]) ut vir ipse. Videor mihi, quotiescunque talia et alia Keppleri lego, nescio quorsum extra me abripi. Seposui haec obscura ad tempus, cum mihi bellum e bello surgat.
