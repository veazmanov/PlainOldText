# Crüger to Müller, August 1623

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the corresponding extract of the original; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

Thank you for sharing Kepler's writings. They are marvels (as that Italian [Magini?, Galilei?] used to say), like the man himself. Whenever I read these and other things of Kepler's, I seem to be carried out of myself, I know not where. I have put these obscure things aside for a time, since for me war is rising out of war.
