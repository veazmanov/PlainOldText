# Crüger to Müller, 12/22 April 1634

::: {.editorial-note}
From Peter Crüger's letter to Philipp Müller written at Danzig on 12/22 April 1634, asking what has become of the papers of Kepler and Bartsch and of Tycho Brahe's observations. The original is in the Bibliothèque de l'Observatoire, Paris (B, 1, 9; 89, 9. Z. 46). Printed in extracts in *Nova Kepleriana* 4, ed. Walther von Dyck and Max Caspar, Abhandlungen der Bayerischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Math.-naturw. Abteilung 31, no. 1 (Munich, 1927), 111.

Transcribed from the page images of the 1927 printing. Only the Latin is given; the editors' German commentary and notes are left out. The 1927 edition prints only extracts. Each paragraph below is one of its extracts, and the omission marks (…) are the editors'. Spaced type, which the editors used for emphasis, is given in italics.
:::

Ubi quaeso jam latent scripta Keppleri, Bartschii et Observationes Tychonis? Quanta jactura perirent! Utinam conveherentur in locum a communibus turbis immunem!
