# Crüger to Müller, 12/22 April 1634

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the corresponding extract of the original; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

Where, I ask you, are the writings of Kepler and Bartsch, and Tycho's observations, hidden now? What a loss it would be if they perished! If only they were brought to some place safe from the common turmoil!
