# Crüger to Müller, Palm Sunday 1628

::: {.editorial-note}
From Peter Crüger's letter to Philipp Müller of Palm Sunday 1628, written at Danzig after he had seen the *Rudolphine Tables* announced in the Frankfurt fair catalogue. The original is in the Bibliothèque de l'Observatoire, Paris (B, 1, 9; 89, 9. M. 34). Printed in extracts in *Nova Kepleriana* 4, ed. Walther von Dyck and Max Caspar, Abhandlungen der Bayerischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Math.-naturw. Abteilung 31, no. 1 (Munich, 1927), 108.

Transcribed from the page images of the 1927 printing. Only the Latin is given; the editors' German commentary and notes are left out. The 1927 edition prints only extracts. Each paragraph below is one of its extracts, and the omission marks (…) are the editors'. Spaced type, which the editors used for emphasis, is given in italics.
:::

Vidi in Catalogo Francofurtano prodiisse Tabulas Keppleri Rudolfinas … Itaque ad Götzium scribo, ut mihi mittantur … Etsi Astronomiae jam parum vaco, tamen in futurum, si Deus hac aestate pacem nobis reddiderit, me praeparabo. Etsi quis Astrophilorum est, qui Tabulas istas, tot annos desideratas, sibi nolit? Ego tanto magis, quanto incertiorem me relinquit Severinus … Itaque desiderio Tabularum Keppleri teneor ingenti.
