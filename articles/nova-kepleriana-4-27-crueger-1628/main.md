# Crüger to Müller, Palm Sunday 1628

::: {.editorial-note}
Only the Latin is given; the editors' German commentary and notes are left out. The 1927 edition prints only extracts. Each paragraph below is one of its extracts, and the omission marks (…) are the editors'. Spaced type, which the editors used for emphasis, is given in italics.
:::

Vidi in Catalogo Francofurtano prodiisse Tabulas Keppleri Rudolfinas … Itaque ad Götzium scribo, ut mihi mittantur … Etsi Astronomiae jam parum vaco, tamen in futurum, si Deus hac aestate pacem nobis reddiderit, me praeparabo. Etsi quis Astrophilorum est, qui Tabulas istas, tot annos desideratas, sibi nolit? Ego tanto magis, quanto incertiorem me relinquit Severinus … Itaque desiderio Tabularum Keppleri teneor ingenti.
