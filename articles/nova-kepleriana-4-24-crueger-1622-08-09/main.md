# Crüger to Müller, 9 August 1622

::: {.editorial-note}
From Peter Crüger's letter to Philipp Müller of 9 August 1622, written at Danzig after he had received the fourth book of Kepler's *Epitome astronomiae Copernicanae* (1620), on the proportions of the planetary orbs and bodies. The original is in the Bibliothèque de l'Observatoire, Paris (B, 1, 9; 89, 9. D. 26). Printed in extracts in *Nova Kepleriana* 4, ed. Walther von Dyck and Max Caspar, Abhandlungen der Bayerischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, Math.-naturw. Abteilung 31, no. 1 (Munich, 1927), 107.

Transcribed from the page images of the 1927 printing. Only the Latin is given; the editors' German commentary and notes are left out. The 1927 edition prints only extracts. Each paragraph below is one of its extracts, and the omission marks (…) are the editors'. Spaced type, which the editors used for emphasis, is given in italics.
:::

Librum Astronomiae Kepplerianae quartum accepi … Legi, nec semel, quae de proportione Orbium et Corporum Planetariorum Kepplerus citatis abs te locis infert. Lectio decies repetita placebit, ait Poëta. Sed haec vel centies repetita nondum intelligo. Et videtur autor more suo rem obscurare de industria … Considerabo tamen posthac per ocium omnia summis viribus, quamquam non video, cui bono. Nituntur enim haec fundamentis lubricis et meris conjecturis. Certiora forte deprehendemus in Astronomia Danica …
