# Crüger to Müller, 9 August 1622

::: {.editorial-note}
English translation of the Latin extracts printed in *Nova Kepleriana* 4 (1927), made for Plain Old Text. Each paragraph translates the extract beside it; the omission marks (…) are the 1927 editors'. Italics follow the editors' emphasis.
:::

I have received the fourth book of Kepler's Astronomy … I have read, and more than once, what Kepler argues about the proportions of the planetary orbs and bodies in the places you cited. “Read ten times over, it will please,” says the Poet. But these things, even read a hundred times over, I still do not understand. And the author seems, as is his way, to obscure the matter on purpose … Still, I shall consider everything hereafter at leisure, with all my strength, though I do not see to what end. For these things rest on slippery foundations and mere conjectures. We shall perhaps find surer things in the *Astronomia Danica* …
